Have Some Questions?

What is your minimum guest requirement?
Minimum 10 guests. Maximum? We cater for up to 300+ guests at home events.
Can the menu be customized?
Absolutely. Veg, non-veg, saatvik, jain, no-onion-garlic — everything is possible. Just let us know.
How far in advance should I book?
24 hours advance preferred, but same-day orders are also possible (subject to availability). Contact us on WhatsApp.
Are there separate delivery charges?
Delivery charges are clearly mentioned in the quote based on your location. There are no hidden charges.
Will the food arrive hot?
Yes. We use heat-retentive premium packaging, so your food arrives oven-fresh.
Are plates, spoons, and napkins included?
Yes. Premium eco-friendly disposables are included in all packages.
Is tasting possible?
Yes! You can order a sample thali or contact us on WhatsApp to arrange a tasting.
What is the advance payment for booking?
50% advance at booking, the rest on delivery. UPI, bank transfer, and cash are accepted.
